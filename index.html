FAQ

Questions we hear often

How many days does it take to learn car driving?

Our car course is 20 days with 1 hour of practice daily. Most learners feel confident by the end.

Do I need a learner's licence (LLR) first?

Yes, you need it to practise on public roads. We help with the online application and slot booking.

What documents are needed for a licence?

Usually age proof, address proof (Aadhaar often works) and passport-size photos. Requirements can vary, so we will give you the exact list when you call.

Are RTO fees included in the course fee?

No. Government / RTO fees are paid separately. We tell you the amount in advance.

Do you have women trainers for women?

Yes. We offer special training for women with women trainers: scooty, gear bike and car.

I forgot how to drive. Can you help?

Yes, our one-day refresher helps you get your confidence back. Call us to book.

Ready to take the driver's seat?
