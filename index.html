FAQ

Questions we hear often

How many days does it take to learn driving?

Most beginners feel comfortable in 15–21 days of daily practice. It depends on how often you practise and how confident you already are.

Do I need a learner licence before I start?

To drive on public roads you need a learner licence. We help you apply right at the start so your practice is legal and safe.

What documents are needed for a licence?

Usually age proof, address proof (Aadhaar often works) and passport-size photos. A medical certificate may be needed depending on your age and state. We will give you the exact list.

Are government (RTO) fees included in the course fee?

No. RTO and test fees are paid separately at actual rates. We tell you the amount in advance.

Can I learn on an automatic car?

Yes. Choose manual or automatic when you enquire, and we will also tell you which suits you better.

What if I do not pass the driving test?

Do not worry, you can re-appear after the waiting period set by the RTO. We help you rebook the slot and practise the weak areas.
